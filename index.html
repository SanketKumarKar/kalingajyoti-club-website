"ଜୟ ଜଗନ୍ନାଥ"

KalingaJyoti is dedicated to preserving and promoting Odisha's rich cultural heritage through dance, music, art, and community events. Founded with a passion for our roots, we create spaces where traditions thrive and community bonds flourish.
